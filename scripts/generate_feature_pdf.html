Project Overview & Purpose (آسان الفاظ میں خلاصہ)

AceOne Solutions Multi-Tenant Meta Lead CRM ek mukammal aur centralized SaaS platform hai jo Meta (Facebook aur Instagram) Lead Ads ko live collect kar ke CRM mein organize karta hai. Yeh aam single-company CRM nahi hai, balkay ek Multi-Tenant SaaS hai jahan AceOne ka Super Admin multiple client companies ko manage karta hai aur har client ko sirf uske assigned Meta Business Portfolio ka data dikhta hai.

Core Architecture (Sab Se Ahem Nukta):
AceOne ka ONE Centralized Meta Developer Account hai jisme multiple Business Portfolios mojood hain (e.g. Portfolio 1: Azwa Company, Portfolio 2: Apex Company). Har client ke liye alag Meta Developer App banane ki zaroorat nahi hai. Super Admin 1-to-1 mapping ke zariye kisi bhi portfolio ko specific client company ko assign kar deta hai.

1. Platform Architecture & Data Isolation (سیکیورٹی اور آئسولیشن)

  • One Centralized Connection: Super Admin ek dafa Meta OAuth se central account connect karta hai. System tamam Business Portfolios ko discover kar leta hai.
  • Strict 1-to-1 Portfolio Assignment: Har client ko sirf ek Business Portfolio assign hota hai. Duplicate assignment system automatically block karta hai taake koi data overlap na ho.
  • OWASP ASVS Multi-Tenant Security: Database queries backend server-side session cookie se client ka tenantId leti hain. Client A kisi bhi soorat Client B ka data, leads ya campaigns nahi dekh sakta.
  • Token Encryption at Rest: Meta user aur page tokens database mein AES-256-GCM encryption ke sath mehfooz rehte hain.

Central Meta account ke tamam Business Portfolios ki table, Meta Business ID, Ad Accounts aur Pages ki ginti, aur client assignment/reassignment workflow.

Page Lead Access diagnostics, webhook subscription check, token validity verification, aur missing permissions ke liye clear resolution guidance.

Webhook Real-time Meta Lead Ingestion

GET verification (hub.challenge) aur POST signature verification (X-Hub-Signature-256). Idempotent leadgen_id processing jo duplicate leads rokta hai.

Webhook Raw Submission Preservation

Customer ke form responses (Name, Phone, Email, Service) ko normalized format ke sath sath original Graph API JSONB format mein hamesha safe rakhta hai.
